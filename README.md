# `scipy-india`/`archive`

This repository is our public archive of past events and materials related to the SciPy India community. It contains relevant resources from previous conferences, workshops, and miscellaneous activities and events.

## Contents

We'll use this section to provide a brief overview of the contents of the repository. This may include:

- **Past event materials**: Slides, recordings, and other resources from previous SciPy India conferences and workshops.
- **Recordings**: Video and audio recordings of talks, panel discussions, and other sessions from past events, such as conferences and community calls.
- **Meeting notes**: Summaries and minutes from community meetings and planning sessions.
- **Miscellaneous resources**: Any other relevant materials that may be of interest to the community.
